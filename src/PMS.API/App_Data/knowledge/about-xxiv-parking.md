# About XXIV Parking

## What XXIV Parking is

XXIV Parking is a chain of parking lots with one app and website. Customers can find a lot,
compare prices and free spaces, see the lot on a 3D map, book a bay for a car, pay online, follow
a parked vehicle and see their parking history and receipts. Anyone can browse lots, prices and
free spaces without an account; booking, the wallet and parking history need a customer account.

## Vehicle types

XXIV Parking serves three vehicle types: 4-seat cars, 7-seat cars and motorbikes. Bicycles,
e-bikes and electric-vehicle charging are not offered. Each lot has its own prices for each
vehicle type.

## Bicycles and electric bikes

XXIV Parking lots do not accept bicycles or electric bikes (e-bikes), and there are no bicycle
racks. Only 4-seat cars, 7-seat cars and motorbikes can park.

## Electric vehicle charging

XXIV Parking lots have no charging stations for electric cars or electric motorbikes. An electric
car or motorbike can still park like any other vehicle of its type, but it cannot be charged.

## Difference between booked bays and walk-in spaces

A lot has two kinds of spaces. Booked bays (reserved bays) are fixed, signposted bays that customers
reserve in advance in the app; each booking has its own numbered bay. Walk-in spaces are for
vehicles that arrive without a booking: a walk-in vehicle is not given a numbered bay in advance,
and the lot only counts how many walk-in spaces are still free in each zone. When the walk-in
spaces of a zone are full, walk-in vehicles of that type cannot enter until a space frees up.

## Finding a lot and checking free spaces

The home page and the customer dashboard list every open lot with its address, prices and the
number of free spaces, updated in real time. Opening a lot shows its 3D map: booked bays are
coloured by status and walk-in areas show how full they are. The directions button shows a map
with the driving route from your position, and a link to open the route in Google Maps.

## When a lot is closed or under maintenance

A lot can be open, under maintenance, temporarily closed or closed for good, and the lot list and
the 3D map show its status. A lot under maintenance can be viewed but does not take bookings. A
temporarily closed lot does not let new vehicles in and does not take bookings, but vehicles
already inside can still leave; it reopens by itself at the reopening time the lot set, and its
future bookings are cancelled with the whole deposit refunded to your wallet. A lot closed for good
does not reopen.

## Why a lot shows as under maintenance

A lot also shows as under maintenance while it has no manager assigned. It can still be viewed on
the map, but it does not take bookings until a manager is assigned and the lot opens again.

## Getting help from staff

For anything the app cannot do, or for a problem at the gate, talk to the staff at the lot's
gate or counter. Staff can look up your vehicle by plate, take payment, handle a lost ticket and
help with a booking.
