# Booking a bay

## How to book a bay

Sign in, open a lot, pick a free booked bay of your vehicle's type on the 3D map, choose the
vehicle and your arrival time, then choose whether to pay a deposit. Only one person can hold a
bay: if someone else takes the same bay first, the app tells you the bay is no longer available
and you pick another one.

## How far ahead you can book

A car bay can be booked for an arrival time up to 30 days ahead, never in the past. The bay stays
free for other drivers until 2 hours before your arrival time, then it is held for you.

## Booking modes: pay-as-you-go and scheduled

A pay-as-you-go booking only has an arrival time: you pay for the time you actually park when you
leave. A scheduled booking also has a departure time, so the bay is planned for a set window of at
least 30 minutes and at most 24 hours. The same bay can be booked by another driver from 30 minutes
after your departure time, so leave on time: a scheduled booking that is still inside after its
departure time loses its deposit.

## Booking a motorbike bay

Motorbike bays are not booked ahead. On a motorbike floor of the 3D map, tap a free bay to claim it
right away, with no time to pick and no deposit. The staff at the entry lane then see your plate,
and you pay the parking fee when you leave.

## Booking with or without a deposit

The deposit is optional. A booking without a deposit is confirmed at once and the bay is held for
15 minutes after your arrival time. A booking with a deposit is confirmed when the deposit is paid
and the bay is held longer: 30 minutes after your arrival time. See the deposits section for
amounts and refunds.

## How many bookings you can have

There are two limits, and both apply:
- An account can have at most 2 active bookings at a time.
- Each vehicle can have at most 1 active booking, so the 2 bookings must be for 2 different vehicles.
A booking stops counting once it is completed, cancelled or expired.

## Checking in with a booking

You can check in from 15 minutes before your arrival time until the hold ends (15 minutes after
arrival without a deposit, 30 minutes with a deposit). At the entry lane, show the booking's QR code
from the app, give the booking code, or let the camera read the plate of your verified vehicle. You
can also press the check-in button of the booking in the app when you arrive: the staff at the entry
lane see your request and open the barrier. The QR code appears once the booking is confirmed (for
a deposit booking, once the deposit is paid).

## Arriving early

If you arrive more than 15 minutes before your arrival time, the booking cannot check in yet. You
can wait until the check-in time opens, or enter as a walk-in vehicle if walk-in spaces are free;
your booking stays valid.

## Arriving late or not coming (no-show)

If you do not check in before the hold ends, the booking becomes a no-show, the bay is released and
a paid deposit is kept. If you arrive after that, you can still enter as a walk-in vehicle when the
lot has free walk-in spaces.

## Changing a booking

A confirmed booking can be changed in the app until 30 minutes before the arrival time: a new
arrival time, a new departure time, a different booking mode or a different bay. After that
deadline the booking can no longer be changed.

## Cancelling a booking

Cancel a booking in the app under My bookings. Cancelling at least 30 minutes before the arrival
time refunds the whole deposit to your XXIV wallet. Cancelling later than that keeps the deposit.
A booking without a deposit can be cancelled with nothing to refund.

## Booking reminders

The app sends a reminder notification 15 minutes before your arrival time and another one 5
minutes before the hold of your bay ends. With a scheduled booking, the app also reminds you 15
minutes before your departure time while your vehicle is still parked.

## Leaving after your scheduled departure time

If a scheduled booking is still inside after its departure time, even by a few minutes, the lot keeps
the whole deposit and you pay the whole parking fee for the real time you parked (the deposit is not
deducted). Leaving on time, or a pay-as-you-go booking, has the deposit deducted from the fee as
usual. A booking without a deposit just pays the extra time by the lot's price list. The app reminds
you 15 minutes before the departure time; if your vehicle is still parked 5 minutes after it, the app
tells you and the lot's staff are told too, because another driver may have booked your bay from 30
minutes after your departure.

## Your booking moved before you arrived

Your bay is held for you from 2 hours before your arrival time. If the bay is out of service at that
moment (for example under maintenance), your booking moves to another free bay of the same type and
the app tells you the new bay. Nothing else about the booking changes. If instead another vehicle is
still in your bay, the bay waits for it to leave; if it is still there when you arrive, the staff
move your booking or compensate you (see the next section).

## When your booked bay is taken by another vehicle

If another vehicle is parked in your booked bay, the app tells you. The booking is moved to another
free bay of the same type, by the system or by the staff, and your first hour (first price block) is
free. If no bay of your type is left, the booking is cancelled, you park in the walk-in area
instead, your whole deposit is refunded to your wallet and a compensation is paid into your wallet.
Each lot sets the compensation in its price list; the standard amount is 100,000 VND for a car and
50,000 VND for a motorbike.

## Why a booking can be refused

Common reasons: the arrival time is in the past or more than 30 days ahead; the vehicle is not on
your account or its papers are still waiting for review; the bay is for another vehicle type or out
of service; the bay is already booked for part of that time; the lot is under maintenance,
temporarily closed or has no manager yet; or you already have the maximum number of active
bookings (2 per account, 1 per vehicle).
