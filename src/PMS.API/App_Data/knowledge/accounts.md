# Customer accounts

## Creating an account

Customers sign up themselves on the Register page with their full name, email, Vietnamese phone
number and a password, or sign in with Google. The email and the phone number must not be used by
another account. A Vietnamese phone number has 10 digits and starts with 0. When signing up, the
customer agrees to how XXIV Parking processes personal data such as plates, gate photos and
vehicle papers.

## Password rules

A password must have at least 6 characters, including at least one upper-case letter and at least
one special character (for example `!`, `@` or `#`). The same rule applies to every account.

## Signing in with Google

A customer can sign in with a Google account. An account created with Google can also set its own
password later, then sign in with email and password too.

## Forgot password

On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password" and enter the account's email. XXIV Parking emails
a link to set a new password. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes; ask for a new one
if it has expired.

## Changing the password or the profile

Signed-in customers change their name, phone number, avatar and password on the Profile page.
Changing the password needs the current password.

## Staff and manager accounts

Staff, manager and admin accounts cannot be created on the Register page. They are created inside
the company: an admin creates managers, and a manager creates staff for their own lots.
