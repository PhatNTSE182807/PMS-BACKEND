# Vehicles and vehicle verification

## Adding a vehicle to your account

On the Vehicles page, add a vehicle with its plate number, vehicle type (4-seat car, 7-seat car or
motorbike) and colour. The page checks while you type that the plate is not already registered by
another account. A customer can keep several vehicles and mark one as the default. A vehicle must
be on your account and approved before you can book a bay or claim a motorbike bay for it.

## Why vehicles are verified

Verification proves that you are allowed to use the plate on your account. A verified vehicle can
be booked, can check in at the gate by its plate, and shows its parking sessions in your history.
Photos of your papers are only used for this check.

## Papers needed for verification

To verify a vehicle, upload a clear photo of the front of the vehicle registration certificate
and a photo of the front of your citizen ID, then submit them for review. Photos can be JPEG, PNG
or PDF, up to 10 MB each. Use the original papers, not a copy, and make sure the plate and the
owner's name can be read.

## How long verification takes

Submitted papers go to the XXIV Parking staff review queue. Staff check that the plate, the
vehicle and the owner's name match the papers. You get a notification in the app as soon as the
vehicle is approved or rejected; reviews are normally done within 24 hours.

## A vehicle waiting for review cannot be booked

While a vehicle's papers are waiting for staff review, that vehicle cannot be booked yet. Wait for
the approval notification, or book with another approved vehicle.

## If verification is rejected

When staff reject the papers, the vehicle is removed from your account and you get a notification
with the reason. The Vehicles page keeps showing the rejected vehicle and its reason for 30 days.
Fix the problem (for example a blurry photo or the wrong paper), then add the vehicle again with
new photos. If you think the decision is wrong, ask the staff at a lot for help.

## One plate, one account

A plate can be registered by only one account in the whole system. If the app says the plate is
already registered by another account, ask the staff at a lot for help.

## Changing the plate or the owner of a vehicle

After the papers of a vehicle are submitted, its plate and owner cannot be edited. Remove the
vehicle and add it again with the right details and new photos.

## How long XXIV Parking keeps your papers

Photos of vehicle papers are kept in private storage that only verification staff can open, and
every view is logged. They are deleted automatically 30 days after the final decision on the
vehicle.

## Removing a vehicle

You can remove a vehicle from your account on the Vehicles page. A vehicle that is parked right now
or that has an active booking cannot be removed until the session or the booking ends.
