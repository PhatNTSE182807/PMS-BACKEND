# Using the XXIV Parking app

## Active Sessions page

The Active Sessions page shows every vehicle of yours that is parked right now and every booking
that has not finished yet, each with its lot, bay, time and status.

## My Bookings page

The My Bookings page lists your bookings. From there you can open a booking's QR code, pay a
pending deposit, change the time, mode or bay before the change deadline, cancel it, or send a
check-in request when you arrive.

## The parked-vehicle card

While your vehicle is parked, the dashboard shows a parked-vehicle card with the lot, the bay, the
time parked and the fee so far after the deposit. From the card you can pay in the app, open the
exit pass, send a check-out request and open directions to the lot. Once a check-out request is
sent, the card no longer offers payment options: pay at the exit lane.

## Check-in request

When you arrive with a booking inside its check-in time, or after claiming a motorbike bay, press
check-in in the app. The staff at the entry lane see your request with your plate and open the
barrier after the usual gate checks. You can cancel a request that is still waiting.

## Check-out request

When you are ready to leave, press check-out on the parked-vehicle card. The staff at the exit lane
see your request. If the fee is not paid yet, pay it at the exit lane; then the staff open the
barrier.

## A gate request was refused

If the staff refuse a check-in or check-out request, the app shows the reason. Follow it, send a new
request, or go to the lane and talk to the staff.

## Directions to a lot

On the Dashboard, open a lot's 3D model (Book Slot) and press View map under the model, next to the
lot's address, to see a full-screen map with the exact location and the driving route from where you
are. The route icon next to each lot's name, and directions on the parked-vehicle card, open the same
map. The Open in Google Maps button opens the destination in Google Maps for turn-by-turn navigation.

## Notifications

The bell in the app shows notifications in real time: vehicle approved or rejected, check-in and
check-out, booking reminders, price changes, a booked bay taken, moved or free again, a booking
cancelled by the lot, deposit refunds and compensations. You can mark notifications as read or
delete them.

## What the chat assistant can help with

Optimus Prime, the chat assistant (the round XXIV logo at the bottom right), answers questions about XXIV
Parking: booking, deposits and refunds, prices and fees, paying and leaving, the wallet, vehicle
verification, lost tickets and your account. It also knows the lots in the system now, with their
address, status, opening hours and current prices, so it can list the lots, compare prices and tell
you where a lot is. It cannot see free spaces, your own bookings, vehicles or payments, and cannot
make changes for you; use the app pages or ask the staff at the lot for that.
