# Paying and leaving the lot

## Where you can pay

You can pay the parking fee in three places: at the exit lane (the barrier opens right after
payment), at the lot's counter, or in the app before you walk back to your vehicle.

## Payment methods

At the exit lane or the counter you can pay in cash or by scanning a payment QR code. In the app
you can pay with your XXIV wallet, VNPay or MoMo. Customers without an account can pay at the exit
lane or the counter.

## Paying in the app before leaving

Open the parked-vehicle card in the app and pay the fee shown. Once paid you get an exit pass (a QR
code) and 15 minutes to leave. Show the exit pass at the exit lane and the barrier opens.

## The 15-minute exit window

After paying in the app or at the counter, you have 15 minutes to drive out. If you leave later,
the fee is calculated again for the whole time from entry to the new exit time, what you already
paid is subtracted, and a late-exit charge of 10,000 VND is added. Paying at the exit lane itself
has no exit window because the barrier opens straight away.

## Exit pass

Every checked-in vehicle of a customer has an exit pass (a QR code) in the app. After the fee is paid, the exit
lane scans the exit pass and opens the barrier. The exit pass is signed, so a copied or edited code
does not work.

## Leaving after paying

Once the fee is paid (in the app, at the counter, or when the fee is 0), your vehicle is put in the
exit lane's queue. Drive to the exit lane: the staff there see your vehicle and open the barrier.
You can also press the check-out button on the parked-vehicle card to tell the exit lane you are
leaving.

## Payment is pending

If a QR or online payment has been made but not confirmed yet, wait about a minute. The staff can
check the payment with the payment provider or take another payment method; if the first payment
then goes through as well, the extra payment is refunded automatically. A VNPay or MoMo payment link
is valid for 15 minutes; after that, start the payment again.

## Vehicles parked for a long time

A vehicle parked for more than 24 hours is flagged as overdue for the staff, and one left for 7 days
is treated as abandoned. The fee keeps running until the vehicle leaves; check the daily cap in the
lot's price list.
