# Prices and parking fees

## Where to see prices

Each lot sets its own prices per vehicle type. Open a lot in the app or on the website to see its
price list. The assistant does not quote prices; always check the lot's price list, which is the
current price.

## How the parking fee is calculated

Parking is charged in time blocks (usually one hour; the lot's price list shows its blocks). The
first block is charged as soon as the vehicle enters, so there is no free period. Each block that
has started is charged in full. Some lots charge a different price for day and night hours; then
each block costs the price of the hour it starts in. Fees are rounded up to the next 1,000 VND.

## Daily cap

Many lots have a daily cap: the most you pay for one calendar day of parking, whatever the number of
hours. The cap restarts every calendar day. The lot's price list shows whether it has one.

## When prices change

A new price starts as soon as the lot saves it. A vehicle already parked keeps the price it entered
with. Every vehicle that enters afterwards pays the new price, including vehicles with a booking
made before the change. Customers get a notification when a lot changes its prices.

## Checking the fee so far

While your vehicle is parked, the parked-vehicle card in the app shows how long it has been parked
and the fee so far, after the deposit.

## Who parks for free

Parking is free only for vehicles on a lot's free list (for example emergency or official vehicles)
or when the lot waives a fee. A vehicle that turns around at the entry without parking pays nothing.

## Receipts

Every finished parking session has an e-receipt in the app with the fee breakdown, the deposit used
and the payments made. Open it from your parking history.
