# Deposits and refunds

## How much the deposit is

The deposit is a fixed amount per vehicle type, set by each lot. The standard deposit for a car is
30,000 VND. The amount is shown before you confirm the booking.

## Paying the deposit

Pay the deposit from your XXIV wallet, or with VNPay or MoMo. The deposit must be paid within 10
minutes of picking the bay; otherwise the booking expires and the bay is released. If a VNPay or
MoMo payment arrives after the booking has already expired, the money is refunded automatically.

## How the deposit is used at check-out

When you leave, the deposit is deducted from the parking fee. If the fee is higher, you pay the
difference. If the fee is lower than the deposit, the rest of the deposit is refunded automatically
to your XXIV wallet. The one exception is a scheduled booking that leaves after its departure time:
then the deposit is kept and the whole fee is paid.

## When the deposit is refunded in full

The whole deposit is refunded to your XXIV wallet when you cancel at least 30 minutes before the
arrival time, when the lot cancels your booking (for example because the lot closes), and when your
booked bay is taken and no other booked bay is left for you.

## When the deposit is kept

The deposit is not refunded when you cancel less than 30 minutes before the arrival time, when you
do not check in before the hold of your bay ends (a no-show), or when a scheduled booking is still
inside after its departure time (then you also pay the whole parking fee).

## Where refunds go

Automatic refunds for bookings (cancellations, the rest of a deposit, a lot cancellation, a taken
bay) go to your XXIV wallet, and the app sends a notification. You can use the wallet balance for
the next payment or withdraw it.

## Paid twice by mistake

If the same parking session is paid twice (for example in the app and again at the exit lane), the
first payment is kept and the second one is refunded through the way it was paid. A cash payment is
given back on the spot by the staff.
