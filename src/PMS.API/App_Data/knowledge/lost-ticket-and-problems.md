# Lost tickets and problems at the gate

## Lost parking ticket

If you lost your parking ticket or card, go to the lot's gate or counter staff. They look up your
vehicle by plate and ask for photos of the driver's citizen ID and the vehicle registration
certificate. When the staff approve the papers, the lot's lost-ticket fine is added to your bill at
check-out and you can leave after paying. If the papers are not approved, the vehicle stays parked
until new papers are uploaded and approved. The fine is set by each lot.

## Customers with the app do not need a paper ticket

A customer whose vehicle is verified, or who checked in with a booking, can use the exit pass in the
app instead of a paper ticket.

## The camera read the wrong plate

The entry and exit lanes read plates with a camera. When the camera is not sure, or the plate at the
exit does not match the plate at the entry, the barrier stays closed and the staff check the vehicle
before letting it through. Stay at the lane and follow the staff's instructions.

## The lot is full

When the walk-in spaces for your vehicle type are full, walk-in vehicles cannot enter. Check another
lot's free spaces in the app, or book a car bay in advance.

## Parking history

Your parking history shows sessions you booked or paid for yourself, and every session of your
verified vehicles from the day they were verified. Sessions from before verification are not shown.

## Notifications

The bell in the app shows notifications such as vehicle approved or rejected, check-in, check-out,
booking reminders, refunds and price changes. Notifications arrive in real time while the app is
open.
