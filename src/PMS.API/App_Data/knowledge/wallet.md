# XXIV wallet

## What the XXIV wallet is

Every customer account has an XXIV wallet in the app. It holds money for deposits and parking fees,
and it receives refunds such as cancelled deposits and the rest of a deposit after check-out.

## Topping up the wallet

Top up the wallet in the app with VNPay or MoMo. The smallest top-up is 10,000 VND. A customer can
top up at most 10,000,000 VND per calendar day (Vietnam time); payment links that are opened but not
paid yet also count toward that limit until they expire.

## Paying with the wallet

The wallet can pay a booking deposit and the parking fee in the app. The balance must cover the
amount; otherwise top up first or choose VNPay or MoMo.

## Withdrawing money from the wallet

Withdraw the wallet balance in the app to a bank account or a MoMo account.

## Wallet history

The wallet screen shows the balance and recent transactions: top-ups, payments, refunds and
withdrawals. Refunds are listed as refunds, with the booking they came from.
